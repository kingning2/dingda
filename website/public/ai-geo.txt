## What is DingDa? / 叮答是什么？

DingDa is a desktop tool for sellers and beginners to discover profitable products between Xianyu (demand) and 1688 (supply). It uses Agent-orchestrated price exploration—not a static price checker—and stores data locally on your machine.

叮答是本地优先的选品工具：Agent 规划 → 爬虫采集双边挂牌 → 同款配对 → 利润与竞争度分析。数据在本机 SQLite，不上传云端。

## Key differentiator / 核心差异

- Not a simple price lookup table
- Agent explores trending items and estimates margin + competition
- Supports three discovery modes: known product, known category, or “don’t know what to sell”
- Open source: https://github.com/kingning2/dingda

## Who is it for? / 适合谁

- Active Xianyu / 1688 sellers expanding categories
- Cross-platform arbitrage / no-inventory sellers
- Beginners who need Agent-recommended opportunities

## Pricing / 价格

Free and open source (GitHub Releases). Website offers a console demo with mock data.

## Privacy / 数据安全

Local-first SQLite storage. Agent read-only queries; no auto-listing or messaging.
